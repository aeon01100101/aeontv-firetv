# AeonTV for Fire TV

AeonTV on the big screen — for Fire TV and Android TV devices.

**Status: foundation build (v0.1.0).** The AeonTV identity is in place —
package `com.aeontv.tv`, AeonTV name, logo and TV banner — on a verified
build of the TV base app. The AeonTV modes are being ported from the
[AeonTV Android app](https://github.com/aeon01100101/aeontv) next:

- **Originally from AeonTV** — Isaiah's own archive.org uploads
- **Other media** — Archive Mode (the full archive.org catalog) and the
  98-channel AeonTV Live TV
- TMDB posters and silent .sphere support, same as on the phone

## Install

Grab the APK from the
[Releases](https://github.com/aeon01100101/aeontv-firetv/releases) page and
sideload it on your Fire TV (it also works on Android TV). Requires
Android 5.0 (API 21) or newer.

## About

AeonTV for Fire TV is based on
[Jellyfin Android TV](https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin-androidtv)
v0.18.11, rebranded and extended by the AeonTV project. Jellyfin client/API
